Model: each cycle, every template strand either gets a primer or re-pairs with its partner, and re-pairing grows as product piles up. Taq finishes at most Taq × 50 nt/s × extension time ÷ length strands per cycle. Each new strand uses one primer. Amplicon length and extension time are illustrative. Other brakes left out: Taq heat loss, Taq bound up by product, pyrophosphate.
