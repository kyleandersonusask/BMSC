2-D PAGE: focus by pI, then separate by size

Dimension 1: isoelectric focusing (left to right). Dimension 2: SDS-PAGE (top to bottom).

Net charge is drawn as a smooth curve that crosses zero at the pI; real titration curves have steps from each ionisable group. Molecules wander by diffusion; any that wander past their pI change sign and are pulled back, which is what keeps a focused band thin. The kick buttons push the tracked protein past its pI by hand. Real focusing takes 12 to 72 hours; here it takes seconds. Proteins are drawn as if visible. Six made-up proteins: A and D are the same size, and B and F have the same pI.
