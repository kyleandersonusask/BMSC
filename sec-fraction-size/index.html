Size exclusion: big fractions hide peaks

The same column run, collected into 2, 5, 10 or 20 tubes. Each tube holds the average of everything that came off while it was filling.

Column: Vt = 30 mL, Vo = 5 mL; K = (Ve − Vo)/(Vt − Vo). Peaks are stylised Gaussians placed like the 1 mL run on the slide (about 8, 15, 20 and 27 mL). A tube's reading is the mean concentration over its volume; peaks are counted where a tube reads higher than both neighbours. Smaller fractions also need uniform packing to stay sharp; this model keeps the packing ideal.
