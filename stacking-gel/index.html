Glycine (pKa 2.3 and 9.6, pI about 6) carries a net −1 about 0.2% of the time at pH 6.8, 5% at 8.3 and 14% at 8.8, roughly 80 times more often in the running gel than in the stacking gel. The dots are lit more often than that so the change shows. Proteins are drawn as if visible; in a real run you see only the dye until you stain. Distances and speeds are stylised. The one-gel clock starts when the stack enters the running gel, so both races run side by side.
